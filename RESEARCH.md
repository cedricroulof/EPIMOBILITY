## The main goal of the Gale-Shapley algorithm
The Gale-Shapley algorithm aims to put a set of single men and single women
together. However, you need to make your best to respect their preferences
in term of men-women.

Why does this matter in our context ? The problem is pretty similar.
Our goal is to providing each student their ideal parter university.
Every student owns their personnal preference :
- the localisation
- the cursus provided
- the level 
- the number of places
- etc...

Consequently, the main goal is to give the best options available to student.
You will find an implementation of this algorithm.
